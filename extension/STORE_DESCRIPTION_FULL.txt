Never be caught off guard by the weather again! Weather Calendar Agent automatically creates, updates, and manages weather-based events in your Google Calendar, keeping you informed about upcoming weather conditions so you can plan ahead.

🌤️ KEY FEATURES

Automatic Weather Alerts:
• 🌧️ Rain Alerts - Get notified when significant rainfall is expected, so you can bring an umbrella and plan indoor activities
• ❄️ Snow Alerts - Stay informed about upcoming snowfall to allow extra travel time and drive safely
• 🌡️ Temperature Alerts - Receive warnings for extreme cold or heat to dress appropriately and stay comfortable
• 💨 Wind Alerts - Get notifications for high wind conditions to secure outdoor items and stay safe
• ☀️ UV Alerts - Receive reminders for sun protection on clear, sunny days
• 🌤️ Perfect Weather Suggestions - Discover ideal days for outdoor activities with perfect temperature and conditions

Smart Automation:
• 🔄 Automatic Updates - Syncs every 15 minutes in the background, keeping your calendar always up-to-date
• 📍 Location Autocomplete - Smart location search with real-time suggestions helps you find your city quickly
• 🌍 Global Support - Works with cities worldwide through OpenWeatherMap's comprehensive database
• 📊 Customizable Units - Choose between Imperial (Fahrenheit, mph) or Metric (Celsius, m/s) based on your preference
• 🔐 Secure Integration - Uses official Google Calendar API with OAuth authentication for maximum security
• 💾 Privacy-Focused - All data stored locally in your browser, no data collection or external servers

HOW IT WORKS

Getting started is simple:
1. Install the extension and click the icon
2. Authorize Google Calendar access (one-time secure OAuth setup)
3. Enter your location and OpenWeatherMap API key (free at openweathermap.org)
4. The extension automatically creates a dedicated "Weather Alerts & Suggestions" calendar
5. Weather events appear automatically and update every 15 minutes

WHAT YOU'LL SEE IN YOUR CALENDAR

The extension creates helpful, detailed events in your Google Calendar:

• "❄️ Cold Weather Alert - 25°F" - Includes temperature range and time-specific alerts (7-9 AM)
• "🌧️ Rain Expected - 8.5mm" - All-day events with rainfall amounts and helpful reminders
• "🔥 Heat Alert - 95°F" - Extended alerts (10 AM-6 PM) with hydration reminders
• "💨 High Winds - 30 mph" - Wind speed alerts with safety recommendations
• "☀️ Perfect Weather Day!" - Highlights ideal conditions for outdoor activities

Each event includes detailed descriptions with specific weather information, making it easy to plan your day accordingly.

REQUIREMENTS

To use this extension, you'll need:
• A Google account with Calendar access
• A free OpenWeatherMap API key (sign up at openweathermap.org - takes 2 minutes)
• Chrome browser

The OpenWeatherMap API key is free and allows up to 60 API calls per minute, which is more than enough for the extension's 15-minute update cycle.
